Method & assumptions (read before use)
This tool builds the ultimate Pu–Mu interaction envelope for a rectangular column / pier section under uniaxial bending and axial load, then checks user-supplied load combinations against it.
  • Strain compatibility (IS 456 Annex E): maximum concrete strain is 0.0035 at the highly compressed edge when the neutral axis lies within the section (xu ≤ D); for xu > D the strain profile passes through 0.002 at 3D/7 from the highly compressed edge.
  • Concrete stress block: parabolic-rectangular per Cl 38.1 (design stress 0.447 fck). Concrete tensile strength is neglected.
  • Reinforcement: idealised design stress-strain curve per IS 456 Fig. 23 for HYSD bars (Fe 415/500/500D/550); elastic-perfectly-plastic at 0.87fy for Fe 250. Displaced concrete area at compression bar locations is deducted.
  • Two branches: the neutral axis depth xu is swept from a shallow depth (tension-controlled) through xu → ∞ (pure axial compression), separately for the +M sense (top face in compression) and −M sense (bottom face in compression) — relevant whenever top/bottom reinforcement differs. Axial capacity is capped at Pu,max = 0.4 fck Ac + 0.67 fy Asc per Cl 39.3.
  • Combined P-M utilisation is computed radially: for each applied (Pu, Mu) point, both P and M are scaled together along the same ratio until the pair lands exactly on the capacity envelope. Utilisation = applied ÷ scaled-capacity — this reflects the true combined effect of axial load and moment acting together, not moment capacity checked in isolation at the applied Pu.
  • Section sketch is schematic: bar count, diameter and vertical cover are to scale; lateral bar spacing is evenly distributed and indicative only, not a detailing drawing.
  • Not covered by this tool — verify separately: minimum eccentricity per Cl 25.4 (emin = unsupported length/500 + D/30, ≥ 20 mm); biaxial bending interaction per Cl 39.6; slenderness / additional moments for slender columns per Cl 39.7; shear, confinement and detailing (Cl 26, IS 13920 for ductile detailing); and section shapes other than rectangular.
  • Scope: single rectangular section, single steel grade for all bars, uniaxial bending about the axis defined by D only. A real column should be checked in both principal directions.
