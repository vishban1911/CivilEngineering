Design Rock Mass Parameters — Hoek-Brown / GSI

Generalized Hoek-Brown criterion (Hoek, Carranza-Torres & Corkum, 2002) · Deformation modulus (Hoek & Diederichs, 2006) · GSI chart (Hoek, Marinos & Cai, updated 2018)
Disclaimer: This tool is provided for preliminary and educational use only. Results should be independently verified by a qualified engineer before use in design or construction. Hoek-Brown/GSI parameter derivation is a widely used empirical method, not a project-specific design code; input selection (σci, GSI, mi, D) requires engineering geological judgement and, wherever practical, site-specific investigation and laboratory testing. This is not a substitute for professional judgment or compliance verification against applicable project-specific code requirements.
